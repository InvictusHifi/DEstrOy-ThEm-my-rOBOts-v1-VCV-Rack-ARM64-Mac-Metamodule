DestroyFX for MetaModule v0.2.0 is an unofficial private-use adaptation for the 4ms MetaModule.

The original Destroy FX project and plugin names are associated with Sophia Poirier and Tom Murphy 7. Their public repository describes and distributes the original desktop effects under GPL version 2.

This MetaModule project:

- is not an official Destroy FX product;
- is not endorsed by the original authors;
- uses a newly written portable DSP and MetaModule wrapper architecture;
- is informed by the publicly documented behaviour of Transverb, Scrubby, Buffer Override, Skidder and Polarizer;
- is not represented as sample-identical to the original desktop plugins;
- is distributed with complete source under GPL-2.0-only.

4ms and MetaModule are names belonging to their respective owner. This private plugin is not an official 4ms release.
